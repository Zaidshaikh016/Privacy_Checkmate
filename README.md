Checkmate is designed to use a combination of local and cloud-based storage.

Some information, such as certain checklist content, preferences, styles, or settings, may be stored directly on your device.

Other information required for online functionality may be stored remotely. This can include account information, social content, shared content, messages, interactions, and other information necessary to provide cloud-based features.
