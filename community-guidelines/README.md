# Checkmate Community Guidelines

Checkmate is a place to share useful checklists, creations, ideas and conversations. These guidelines apply to public posts, listings, comments and other community interactions.

## Respect other people

- Do not bully, harass, threaten or target people with abuse.
- Do not promote hatred or discrimination against people or groups.
- Avoid vulgar, sexually explicit or degrading language and content.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's private information without permission.

## Share safely and honestly

- Do not promote illegal activity, dangerous wrongdoing, or the sale of illegal drugs.
- Do not post scams, impersonation, misleading promotions, repetitive spam or malicious links.
- Only share content you have permission to use. Respect other people's work and intellectual property.
- Keep contributions relevant and suitable for the Checkmate community.

## Context matters

An automated word filter helps flag comments that may violate these guidelines. It can sometimes flag a harmless quotation, an educational discussion or a word used in a different context. A flagged word does not, by itself, prove that someone has broken the rules.

A comment may be hidden from other users while it is checked. Moderators may also remove a post or comment after reviewing it. A notice saying **"Your comment may violate our community guidelines"** or **"Your post may violate our community guidelines"** explains that the content is hidden and offers a way to ask for a review.

## Appeals

If you think a decision missed the context, use **Appeal decision** in the notice. Automated comment notices appear with your comment; notices for moderator removals marked as guideline violations appear privately in your Checkmate Alerts.

Explain the context and why you think the content should be restored. Your original content and explanation are sent to the moderation team. The content stays hidden while the appeal is pending. You can see the review outcome and the moderator's explanation. An approved appeal restores eligible content; a rejected appeal keeps it hidden.

Do not repost the same content repeatedly to get around moderation. Serious or repeated violations may lead to warnings, account restrictions or account removal.

## An evolving policy

**More guidelines will be added when necessary.** We may clarify or update this page as new situations arise, to keep Checkmate useful, safe and fair. Please check back for the latest guidance.
